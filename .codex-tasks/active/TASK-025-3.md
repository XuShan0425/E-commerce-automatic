## Allowed Files

- `.claude/settings.json`
- `project/.claude/settings.json`

- `skills/`
- `docs/`

1. `.claude/settings.json` permissions.allow 包含 30+ 条 (`Bash(*)`, `Read(*)`, `Edit(*)`, `Write(*)`, `Workflow(*)`, `EnterWorktree(*)` 等)
2. `project/.claude/settings.json` 与根目录同步
3. 所有 `schema` 使用 `type: object`（适配 Workflow 工具约束）
